Implement a visual upgrade for the CURRENTS page/background.

Goal:
Replace the current blurry/nondescript CURRENTS backdrop with a photorealistic, premium tropical water movement theme — green-blue ocean / clear river water / Gulf of Mexico vacation energy — while preserving excellent UI/UX readability, performance, accessibility, and load speed.

Important product vibe:
CURRENTS should feel like:
- tropical water movement
- clean Gulf of Mexico / clear river shimmer
- premium, modern, calming, beautiful
- “I’m on holiday even while I’m at work”
- not cartoonish, not cheesy, not visually noisy

Design requirements:
1. Background should be photorealistic-looking tropical water movement.
2. Use green/blue/aqua tones with subtle sunlight shimmer and flowing movement.
3. Avoid blurry abstract blobs. It should look like real moving water.
4. Do NOT let the background interfere with content readability.
5. Add a dark translucent overlay / gradient layer over the moving water so text, cards, buttons, and channel content remain easy to read.
6. Keep CURRENTS feeling professional, not like a beach party flyer. Tropical, but enterprise-grade.
7. Use subtle animation only. No aggressive motion. No distracting waves.
8. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
9. Ensure page load stays fast. Do not use giant unoptimized assets or anything that makes the page slow.
10. Preserve all existing CURRENTS functionality, routing, permissions, channels, messages, modals, and APIs.

Implementation approach:
- Find the CURRENTS page/layout component and its current backdrop/screensaver/background implementation.
- Replace the current blurry background with a reusable CSS-based animated water layer where possible.
- If an image/video asset is needed, use an optimized lightweight asset and lazy-load/preload appropriately.
- Prefer CSS gradients, layered radial highlights, animated background-position, SVG/noise overlays, or a lightweight looping video only if already supported and performant.
- Add a tasteful overlay:
  - dark navy/teal glass layer
  - subtle vignette at edges
  - content panels should remain readable with sufficient contrast
- Add `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that pauses or significantly reduces background movement.
- Ensure the animated background does not block clicks, scrolls, or keyboard focus.
- Set pointer-events: none on decorative layers.
- Keep z-index clean:
  - water background lowest
  - overlay above background
  - actual UI above overlay
- Do not introduce layout shift.

Suggested visual language:
- Deep teal base
- Aqua/emerald moving highlights
- Sunlit caustic shimmer
- Soft current-like horizontal/diagonal movement
- Slight parallax feel
- Premium glassmorphism over the water, but not excessive blur

UI/UX guardrails:
- Text contrast must stay strong.
- Channel list, message list, input box, and modals must remain readable.
- Do not put high-detail water directly behind dense text without an overlay.
- Do not animate inside cards or behind tiny text areas too aggressively.
- Avoid pure white text on bright aqua without a dark scrim.
- Keep motion slow and relaxing.
- Maintain responsive behavior on desktop and mobile.
- Avoid making the page feel visually heavier than VoltSafe Mail or the rest of the CMS.

Acceptance criteria:
1. CURRENTS background is visibly upgraded from blurry/abstract to beautiful photorealistic tropical moving water.
2. The page still loads quickly and feels instant.
3. All readable content remains readable.
4. Animation is subtle and tasteful.
5. Reduced-motion users get a still or near-still version.
6. No regressions to channels, private/public channel creation, messaging, navigation, modals, or permissions.
7. The design feels premium SaaS, not vacation brochure chaos.
