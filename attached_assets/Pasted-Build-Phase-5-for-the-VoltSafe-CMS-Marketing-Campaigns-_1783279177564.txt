Goal:
Build account-level intelligence that helps VoltSafe identify which marina accounts are heating up and which stakeholders inside each marina are engaging.

This phase should answer:

* Which marina accounts are most engaged?
* Which marinas should sales follow up with now?
* Which buying committee members are active?
* Which stakeholder roles are engaging?
* Which campaign created the heat?
* Why is an account hot?
* What should sales do next?

Core concept:
Create an Account Heat Score from campaign engagement + CRM fit signals.

Do not invent fake heat scores. Use real CRM/account/contact/campaign data only.

1. Account Heat Score

Create backend logic that calculates Account Heat Score from 0–100.

Suggested scoring model:

Engagement:

* Unique open: +3
* Total opens beyond first: +1 each, capped at +10
* Unique click: +8
* Total clicks beyond first: +2 each, capped at +12
* Reply: +20
* Meeting/demo booked if data exists: +30
* Multiple engaged contacts at same account: +15
* Engagement in last 7 days: +15
* Engagement in last 30 days: +8

Stakeholder role:

* Owner/CEO/President engaged: +15
* GM/Marina Manager engaged: +12
* Harbormaster/Dockmaster engaged: +10
* Marine Electrician/Facilities engaged: +8
* Deckhand/staff engagement: +3

Fit:

* Adoption readiness score 80+: +15
* Adoption readiness score 60–79: +10
* Premium Independent Marina: +12
* Marina Group / Multi-Site Operator: +15
* Resort / Destination Marina: +12
* Developer / New Build: +12
* Port Authority Marina: +10
* Municipal Marina: +5

Negative signals:

* Unsubscribed contact at account: -10
* Suppressed domain: -30
* No engagement after 3+ sent emails: -10
* Mom & Pop Marina: -5 unless engagement is strong
* Low adoption readiness score: -10

Cap score at 100 and floor at 0.

Heat labels:

* 80–100: Hot
* 60–79: Warm
* 40–59: Nurture
* 20–39: Low
* 0–19: Cold / Do Not Chase Yet
