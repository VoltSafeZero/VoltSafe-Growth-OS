The EML comparison confirms the CTA thumbnail shrinking bug.

Bad/tiny email CTA HTML:
<img src="cid:vsig1mq5excxl" alt="Watch Demo" width="180" style="display:block;border:0;outline:none;text-decoration:none;max-width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;">

Good/normal email CTA HTML:
<img src="cid:vsig1mq5kc2d4" alt="Watch Demo" width="200" style="display:block;border:0;outline:none;text-decoration:none;max-width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;">

Also, the bad email's VoltSafe logo image had no explicit style:
<img src="cid:vsig0mq5excxl">

The good email's logo image had:
<img src="cid:vsig0mq5kc2d4" style="width: 200px;">

Root cause:
The signature HTML generation is inconsistent. The CTA image is sometimes generated at width="180" and sometimes width="200". Worse, the CTA image uses max-width:100% while the parent CTA <td> has no explicit width. In recipient inboxes/reply threads, the parent cell can collapse, causing max-width:100% to shrink the CTA image.

Fix this permanently.
