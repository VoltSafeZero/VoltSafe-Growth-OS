Build VoltSafe Revenue Intelligence System (Phase 4)

Objective:

Transform VoltSafe Mail from an email client with engagement tracking into a revenue intelligence platform that automatically identifies:

- Who is engaging
- Who matters most
- Who is likely the champion
- Who is part of the buying committee
- Which accounts need attention
- Which opportunities should be followed up
- Which relationships are accelerating or cooling

Use the existing recipient-level engagement tracking as the foundation.

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

Use existing systems as the source of truth:

- InboxSignalBadge
- ThreadEngagementWidget
- Needs Reply — High Engagement widget
- Recipient-level tracking
- email_recipients
- email_engagement_events
- email_tracking_pixels

Do NOT create a separate engagement engine.

Everything must use the same engagement scoring source.

==================================================

Goal:

Automatically identify everyone participating in an opportunity.

For each account:

Detect:

- Champion
- Decision Maker
- Stakeholder
- Observer
- Internal Team

Inputs:

- TO / CC / BCC
- Opens
- Clicks
- CTA activity
- Demo activity
- Replies
- Meeting activity
- CRM roles if available

87%
