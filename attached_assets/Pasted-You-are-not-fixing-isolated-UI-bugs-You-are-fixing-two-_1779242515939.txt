REAL EMAIL MODEL

A Gmail thread/message may exist in MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS simultaneously.

Example:
Scott sends email
Trevor is CC’d

System may ingest:
- Scott’s SENT copy
- Trevor’s INBOX copy

These are NOT the same mailbox state.

CRITICAL RULE

FOLDER/DIRECTION MUST ALWAYS BE CALCULATED FROM THE PERSPECTIVE OF THE CURRENT MAIL ACCOUNT.

NOT:
- global sender
- company domain
- sender email alone
- raw thread
- first synced copy

This is the root conceptual bug.

CURRENT FAILURE MODE

System is:
- mixing mailbox perspectives
- leaking SENT labels across accounts
- treating cross-account internal mail as outbound
- overwriting label state during sync
- losing messages during CRM Review transitions

5. Search
