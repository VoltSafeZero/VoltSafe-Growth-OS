Update the AI Suggested Email Generator prompt/instructions so it strongly prioritizes the most recent CRM activity when generating the next email.

Goal:
The AI must generate the next best email based on the newest and most relevant context from the Lead/Account/Contact file, including linked emails, comments, notes, action items, meeting summaries, documents, status fields, and pipeline stage.

Add or replace the email generation instruction with the following:

---
RECENCY-WEIGHTED EMAIL GENERATION RULES

When generating a suggested follow-up email, treat the CRM file as a timeline, not a pile of facts.

The newest relevant activity must carry the most weight.

Priority order:

1. Most recent direct emails
- Prioritize the newest inbound and outbound emails above older notes or summaries.
- The latest reply, question, objection, commitment, requested follow-up, or open loop is the strongest signal.
- Never ignore the most recent email thread.
- Do not restart the conversation as if prior emails did not happen.

2. Most recent comments, notes, meeting summaries, and action items
- Recent internal comments and notes should guide the purpose of the next email.
- If a recent comment says the user plans to reach out today, the generated email should follow that direction.
- If there is a recent action item, the email should move that action forward.

3. Current CRM status and pipeline context
- Use the current pipeline stage, lead status, temperature, confidence, assigned owner, and next-step fields to shape tone and urgency.
- Do not write a generic sales email if the lead is already qualified, warm, active, or mid-conversation.

4. Key people and stakeholder context
- Address the right person or group based on the latest communication.
- Mention relevant stakeholders only when useful.
- Avoid using stale or low-confidence contact details.

5. Older background information
- Older notes should only support the email.
- Older context must not override newer emails, comments, or action items.
- If old and new context conflict, follow the newest relevant activity.

Hard rule:
MOST RECENT ACTIVITY WINS.

A comment, email, note, or action item from today should outweigh a meeting summary from last month unless the older item is explicitly marked as still active, strategic, or unresolved.

Before writing the email, silently determine:
- What happened most recently?
- Who said what last?
- What is the latest open loop?
- What is the most logical next step?
- What should the recipient understand, feel, and do after reading this email?

The generated email must:
- Acknowledge the most recent relevant interaction.
- Explain the reason for reaching out now.
- Use the most relevant value point based on the account context.
- Include one clear next step or call to action.
- Be concise, executive, helpful, and action-oriented.

Avoid:
- Generic “just checking in” emails.
- Repeating stale meeting notes.
- Summarizing the whole account.
- Overexplaining.
- Making claims not supported by the CRM file.
- Ignoring recent emails, comments, or action items.

If the latest context includes a pilot invitation, meeting follow-up, requested deck, compliance question, pricing discussion, technical question, stakeholder intro, scheduling need, or decision deadline, the email must directly advance that item.

If the context is incomplete or conflicting, generate the safest useful email based on the newest reliable activity.
---

Implementation requirement:
Make sure the AI Email Generator receives CRM context sorted newest-first wherever possible.

Context passed into the model should clearly label each item with:
- type: email, comment, note, meeting, action_item, document, CRM field, etc.
- direction: inbound, outbound, internal, if applicable
- timestamp
- author/sender
- recipients, if applicable
- content/body/snippet

Also add a small pre-processing step before calling the model:
- Sort all activity by timestamp descending.
- Mark the newest 3 to 5 relevant activities as HIGH PRIORITY CONTEXT.
- Include a clear instruction that HIGH PRIORITY CONTEXT must dominate the suggested email.

Add tests or verification to confirm:
1. A recent comment overrides older notes.
2. A recent inbound email drives the generated next step.
3. Older meeting notes are used only as supporting context.
4. The generated email does not ignore the newest linked email.
5. The generated email does not produce a generic check-in when a specific recent action item exists.
