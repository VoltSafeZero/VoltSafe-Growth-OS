====================================================
REVERSIBLE CONVERSION / PROMOTION SAFETY
====================================================

Lead <-> Account transitions must be reversible without data loss.

Important:
A Lead becoming an Account is NOT a destructive replacement.
An Account reverting back into a Lead is NOT a destructive replacement.

These are lifecycle/state transitions, not entity deletion/recreation events.

Critical rules:
1. Never delete the original Lead when converting to Account.
2. Never delete the original Account when reverting to Lead.
3. Never recreate Contacts during transitions if they already exist.
4. Never duplicate email associations unnecessarily.
5. Never orphan activities, notes, tasks, or email threads.
6. Preserve all historical relationships and audit history.
7. Preserve all IDs whenever possible.
8. Preserve all timestamps/history.
9. Preserve all linked CRM entity references.
10. Preserve all AI summaries, notes, activity logs, and thread associations.

Required architecture:
Use a durable relationship/mapping model instead of destructive conversion logic.

Examples:
- Lead promoted to Account
- Account demoted/reverted to Lead
- Lead promoted to Partner
- Partner converted to Customer
- Parent Group converted into active Customer account
- Marina Prospect converted into Marina Customer

These should all preserve:
- emails
- contacts
- activities
- tasks
- notes
- AI summaries
- linked opportunities
- linked organizations
- thread associations
- source attribution
- historical audit trail

Implementation guidance:
Treat lifecycle transitions as:
- status/state evolution
NOT:
- delete/recreate operations

Recommended approach:
- Preserve source entity IDs
- Maintain conversion mapping tables
- Maintain bidirectional references
- Maintain historical entity lineage

Example lineage:
Lead #381
 -> promoted to Account #92
 -> reverted to Lead #381 state
 -> promoted again to Account #92

This should NOT:
- duplicate contacts
- lose emails
- break URLs
- lose notes
- lose activity history
- create orphaned associations

====================================================
REQUIRED REVERSIBILITY TESTS
====================================================

1. Lead -> Account preserves emails
2. Lead -> Account preserves contacts
3. Lead -> Account preserves notes
4. Lead -> Account preserves activities
5. Lead -> Account preserves tasks
6. Account -> Lead preserves emails
7. Account -> Lead preserves contacts
8. Account -> Lead preserves notes
9. Account -> Lead preserves activities
10. Account -> Lead preserves tasks
11. Lead -> Account -> Lead roundtrip causes zero data loss
12. Account -> Lead -> Account roundtrip causes zero data loss
13. No duplicate contacts created during roundtrip
14. No duplicate email associations created during roundtrip
15. No orphaned records created during roundtrip
16. Existing URLs/deep links continue working after transitions
17. Existing thread associations remain intact
18. Existing AI summaries remain linked correctly
19. Historical audit trail remains readable
20. Conversion lineage/history is preserved and queryable

====================================================
ENTITY LINEAGE / HISTORY REQUIREMENTS
====================================================

Add a durable lineage/history system if needed:

Examples:
- sourceLeadId
- sourceAccountId
- previousLifecycleState
- promotedAt
- revertedAt
- lifecycleTransitionHistory
- entityLineageId

Goal:
Users should be able to see:
- what this entity used to be
- when it transitioned
- who performed the transition
- what links/data were preserved

====================================================
IMPORTANT UX RULE
====================================================

Users should experience transitions as:
"this record evolved"

NOT:
"this record disappeared and a new one appeared"

The CRM should feel continuous and relational, not destructive.
