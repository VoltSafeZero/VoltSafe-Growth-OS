2. Engagement scoring service

Create a pure backend helper/service:

server/services/capital-engagement.ts

It should compute engagement from transparent signals.

Suggested score components:
- recent inbound email reply
- linked email thread activity
- portal opened
- number of portal opens
- material viewed
- material downloaded
- high-value material viewed, such as pitch deck or financial model
- material request made
- meeting/call activity
- stage advancement
- commitment or verbal commitment
- stale follow-up penalty
- no activity penalty
- portal access not opened penalty
- do_not_contact override

Return:
- engagement_score 0–100
- engagement_tier:
  - Highly Engaged
  - Engaged
  - Watching
  - Stale
  - Cold
- reasons array
- risk_flags array
- recommended_next_action
- last_meaningful_engagement_at
- signal_breakdown

Keep scoring simple and explainable. No black-box magic.
