Build and integrate a CRM email marketing module for VoltSafe Inc. that supports compliant email campaigns in both Canada and the United States.

Context:
VoltSafe is a Canadian company that will send marketing and sales emails to contacts in Canada and to marinas located in the United States. The CRM must support jurisdiction-based compliance rules.

Canada:
Canadian commercial email must comply with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation, CASL. CASL generally requires valid consent before sending a commercial electronic message. Consent can be express or implied. Commercial messages must also identify the sender and include a working unsubscribe mechanism.

United States:
U.S. commercial email must comply with the CAN-SPAM Act. U.S. commercial B2B email is generally opt-out based, not prior-consent based like Canada. However, emails must have accurate sender information, non-deceptive subject lines, a valid physical postal address, and a clear unsubscribe mechanism. Opt-outs must be honoured.

Primary goal:
Build the module so VoltSafe can run email campaigns confidently while the system automatically blocks non-compliant sends.

Core compliance principle:
No valid permission path, no send.
No sender identity, no send.
No physical address, no send.
No unsubscribe mechanism, no send.
No sending to unsubscribed or suppressed contacts, ever.

Brand principle:
Build this like a circuit breaker for marketing. It should make compliant sending easy and non-compliant sending impossible.

Definitions:
A commercial email campaign is any email that promotes, markets, sells, invites, encourages commercial activity, announces products, offers demos, sends marina sales material, promotes VoltSafe Marine, promotes the Premier Access Package, shares commercial event invitations, or otherwise supports business development.

Transactional or service emails are not marketing emails. Examples include password resets, invoices, account security notices, service alerts, safety notices, warranty notices, outage notices, order confirmations, and operational pedestal/system alerts. Do not allow promotional content inside transactional templates.

Canada consent rules:
Support express consent and implied consent for Canadian contacts.

Express consent:

* Express consent is the preferred consent state.
* Express consent must require an affirmative action from the recipient.
* Website forms must use an unchecked checkbox by default.
* Store the exact consent wording displayed to the user.
* Store timestamp, source, form URL, IP address, user agent, and referrer when available.
* Express consent remains active unless the recipient unsubscribes or is suppressed.

Example express consent checkbox copy:
“I agree to receive emails from VoltSafe Inc. about VoltSafe Marine products, marina electrification, launch updates, events, and related commercial opportunities. I understand I can unsubscribe at any time.”

Implied consent:
Support implied consent only with documented proof and expiry logic.

Common implied consent sources:

* Existing customer relationship
* Purchase order
* Active contract
* Product inquiry
* Quote request
* Demo request
* Trade show badge scan where the discussion was relevant to VoltSafe
* Business card provided in a relevant commercial context

Recommended expiry rules:

* Inquiry, demo request, quote request, or contact form without express marketing consent: implied consent expires 6 months after the relationship date.
* Customer, purchase order, paid account, or contract relationship: implied consent expires 24 months after the last transaction, purchase, or contract end date unless renewed or converted to express consent.
* Business card or event scan: implied consent expires 6 months after the event date unless converted to express consent.
* If implied_consent_expiry_date is in the past, automatically set consent_status to implied_expired and prevent Canadian marketing sends.

Do not rely on purchased lists, scraped lists, or contacts with unknown consent for Canada.

Canada send eligibility logic:
Before sending any commercial campaign to Canadian recipients, run a compliance preflight check for every recipient.

A Canadian recipient is sendable only if:

(
consent_status = express_active
OR
consent_status = implied_active AND implied_consent_expiry_date >= today
)
AND unsubscribe_status != unsubscribed
AND suppression_status = none
AND email_valid = true
AND sender_identity_present = true
AND physical_postal_address_present = true
AND unsubscribe_link_present = true

Block Canadian commercial sending if:

* consent_status is unknown
* consent_status is implied_expired
* consent_status is unsubscribed
* consent_status is suppressed or blocked
* unsubscribe_status is unsubscribed
* suppression_status is not none
* email_valid is false
* consent proof is missing
* implied consent has no expiry date
* the campaign has no unsubscribe link
* the campaign has no sender identity
* the campaign has no physical mailing address
* the campaign has no valid contact email or phone number
* the campaign is commercial and has not been classified
* the sender tries to bypass the compliance check

U.S. campaign requirements:

1. Accurate sender identity:

* From name must accurately identify VoltSafe Inc. or a real VoltSafe representative.
* Reply-to email must be valid.
* Sending domain must be approved and associated with VoltSafe.

Good:
From: Trevor Burgess, VoltSafe
Reply-To: trevor@voltsafe.com

Bad:
From: Marina Safety Office
Reply-To: randomdomain123.com

2. No deceptive subject lines:

* Subject line must accurately reflect the email content.
* Flag risky phrases such as “urgent compliance notice,” “invoice,” “renewal,” “required action,” “safety violation,” or “final notice” unless the email is truly transactional, legally required, or accurately described.

Good:
“A safer shore power option for [Marina Name]”

Risky:
“Urgent electrical compliance notice”

3. Advertisement/commercial disclosure:

* Include a footer line for cold U.S. outreach:
    “This is a commercial message from VoltSafe Inc. about marina shore power technology.”

4. Physical postal address:

* Every U.S. commercial email must include VoltSafe’s valid physical postal address.

5. Unsubscribe:

* Every U.S. commercial email must include a clear unsubscribe link.
* Unsubscribe must not require login.
* Unsubscribe must not require payment.
* Unsubscribe must not require extra personal information beyond email address.
* One-click unsubscribe is preferred.

6. Opt-out handling:

* When a U.S. recipient unsubscribes, immediately set:
    unsubscribe_status = unsubscribed
    suppression_status = suppressed
    suppression_reason = unsubscribed
    consent_status = opted_out
* Block all future commercial emails to that address.
* Honour opt-outs immediately.

7. Unsubscribe link duration:

* The unsubscribe mechanism must remain functional for at least 30 days after the campaign is sent.

8. Suppression list:

* Global suppression must override all U.S. campaigns, automations, imports, and manual sends.
* No admin may send commercial emails to suppressed or unsubscribed contacts.

9. Vendor responsibility:

* If an external sender, email platform, agency, SDR tool, or API sends on VoltSafe’s behalf, log the vendor name and campaign source.
* Apply the same CAN-SPAM preflight rules to vendor/API-driven sends.
