Goal:
Build automated campaign sequence scheduling so enrolled recipients can move through campaign email steps automatically, safely, and compliantly.

This phase should turn:
“Manually send Step 1, then manually send Step 2 later”
into:
“Start sequence, and VoltSafe CMS sends each step on schedule only if the recipient remains eligible.”

Core principle:
Automation must be fail-closed. Before every scheduled send, re-check suppression, compliance, unsubscribe status, campaign status, recipient status, and duplicate-send protection.

Do not remove manual sending. Automated scheduling should sit beside it.

2. Automation Rules

A campaign can only start automation if:

* campaign status is active or scheduled
* campaign compliance_status is preflight_passed
* campaign has at least one enrolled recipient
* campaign has at least one campaign email step
* sender identity exists
* campaign is not archived/completed
* user has permission

A recipient can only receive an automated step if:

* recipient is enrolled/active
* recipient is not unsubscribed
* recipient is not suppressed
* recipient has valid email
* recipient has not already received that step
* recipient passes send-time compliance
* campaign passes send-time compliance
* current time is >= next_step_due_at

3. Sequence Timing

Use campaign_emails.delay_days to schedule each step.

Example:

* Step 1 delay_days = 0 → send immediately after automation starts
* Step 2 delay_days = 4 → due 4 days after Step 1 sent
* Step 3 delay_days = 9 → due 9 days after Step 1 sent, or according to existing delay convention

Choose one convention and document it clearly.

Recommended convention:
delay_days is relative to sequence start date, not previous step. This avoids drift and makes campaign schedules easier to reason about.

Example:

* sequence_started_at = July 1
* Step 1 delay_days 0 → July 1
* Step 2 delay_days 4 → July 5
* Step 3 delay_days 9 → July 10

4. Backend Service

Create:

server/services/campaign-automation.ts

Functions:

* validateAutomationStart(campaignId)
* startCampaignAutomation(campaignId, userId)
* pauseCampaignAutomation(campaignId, userId)
* resumeCampaignAutomation(campaignId, userId)
* stopCampaignAutomation(campaignId, userId)
* getCampaignAutomationStatus(campaignId)
* runCampaignAutomationTick(options)

runCampaignAutomationTick should:

* find campaigns with automation_status = active
* find recipients with next_step_due_at <= now
* for each due recipient, determine the next unsent campaign email step
* call existing send logic or shared send helper
* enforce all send-time rules
* create campaign_events:
    * automation_started
    * automation_paused
    * automation_resumed
    * automation_completed
    * automation_step_due
    * automation_step_sent
    * automation_step_skipped
    * automation_step_failed
* update recipient next_step_due_at
* mark recipient completed when final step sent
* mark campaign completed when all eligible recipients complete or are terminal
* never send duplicate steps

Important:
Do not bypass existing compliance gate or send-time renderer. Use the same safe path as manual send wherever possible.

10. Safety / Compliance

Before every automated send:

* re-run or verify compliance preflight
* verify compliance_status is preflight_passed
* re-check recipient suppression
* re-check unsubscribe status
* re-check campaign_suppression domain/email
* verify footer/unsubscribe link can be generated
* verify no unresolved placeholders
* verify no duplicate sent event for same campaign_email_id/recipient
* fail closed if uncertainty exists

If preflight expires or campaign compliance fields change, automation should become blocked until preflight is rerun.

If recipient becomes unsubscribed/suppressed mid-sequence, skip future steps and mark recipient terminal.
