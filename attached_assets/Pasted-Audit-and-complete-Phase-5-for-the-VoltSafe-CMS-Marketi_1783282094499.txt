3. Scoring Model

Use this scoring model:

Engagement:

* Unique open: +3
* Total opens beyond first: +1 each, capped at +10
* Unique click: +8
* Total clicks beyond first: +2 each, capped at +12
* Reply: +20
* Meeting/demo booked if data exists: +30
* Multiple engaged contacts at same account: +15
* Engagement in last 7 days: +15
* Engagement in last 30 days: +8

Stakeholder role:

* Owner/CEO/President engaged: +15
* GM/Marina Manager engaged: +12
* Harbormaster/Dockmaster engaged: +10
* Marine Electrician/Facilities engaged: +8
* Deckhand/staff engagement: +3

Fit:

* Adoption readiness score 80+: +15
* Adoption readiness score 60–79: +10
* Premium Independent Marina: +12
* Marina Group / Multi-Site Operator: +15
* Resort / Destination Marina: +12
* Developer / New Build: +12
* Port Authority Marina: +10
* Municipal Marina: +5

Compliance/risk:

* Unsubscribed contact at account: -10
* Suppressed email/domain at account: -30
* Spam complaint tied to account: -40
* Consent expired for key contact: -15
* Do-not-email contact engaged previously: -20
* No engagement after 3+ sent emails: -10
* Mom & Pop Marina: -5 unless engagement is strong
* Low adoption readiness score: -10

Cap score at 100 and floor at 0.

Heat labels:

* 80–100: Hot
* 60–79: Warm
* 40–59: Nurture
* 20–39: Low
* 0–19: Cold / Do Not Chase Yet
