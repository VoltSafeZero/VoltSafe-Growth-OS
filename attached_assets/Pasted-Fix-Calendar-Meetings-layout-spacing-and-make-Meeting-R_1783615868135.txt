Fix Calendar & Meetings layout spacing and make Meeting Recorder prominent.

Context:
On the Calendar & Meetings page, the "Meetings & Recorder" section appears far below the day calendar with a large empty vertical gap when the daily calendar has few/no events. It should sit immediately below the calendar content and dynamically adjust based on the actual calendar height.

Also, the Meeting Recorder itself does not stand out enough compared to the meeting/recording history list. The recorder action area should be visually prominent and clearly separated above the history.

Required fixes:

A) Calendar → Meetings & Recorder vertical spacing
- Remove any fixed/min-height behavior that forces the day calendar to consume most of the page height when there are few events.
- The Meetings & Recorder section should always render immediately below the bottom of the visible calendar block.
- The layout must dynamically adjust for:
  - empty day calendar
  - lightly scheduled day
  - busy day
  - month/week/day views
  - different browser heights
- Do not leave a large blank area between the calendar grid and Meetings & Recorder.
- If the calendar needs internal scrolling for very busy days, keep that scroll inside the calendar area instead of pushing Meetings & Recorder miles down the page.
- The right sidebar with Today’s Agenda, calendars, and tasks should remain aligned and usable.

Implementation guidance:
- Find the Calendar & Meetings page/component.
- Look for classes/styles like:
  - min-h-screen
  - h-screen
  - min-h-[...]
  - h-[...]
  - flex-1
  - grow
  - overflow-hidden
  - calendar container fixed height
  - large padding/margin before Meetings & Recorder
- Replace rigid height with content-aware sizing.
- Suggested behavior:
  - Calendar shell uses auto height.
  - Day grid has a reasonable max-height only when necessary.
  - Empty/light days collapse closer to actual visible content.
  - Meetings & Recorder has margin-top around 16–24px, not hundreds of pixels.

B) Meeting Recorder prominence
The recorder control/action area must stand out strongly above the history.

Create a dedicated "Meeting Recorder" hero/action card at the top of the Meetings & Recorder section.

It should include:
- Clear title: "Meeting Recorder"
- Short subtitle: "Record, upload, transcribe, and summarize meetings."
- Primary CTA button:
  - "Start Recording" or "New Meeting Note"
- Secondary CTA if supported:
  - "Upload Recording"
- Stronger visual contrast than the history list:
  - slightly tinted background
  - stronger border
  - subtle shadow
  - larger icon
  - clear spacing
- This action card must be visually separate from the recording history.

C) Recording history separation
- Put previous recordings/history below the recorder card.
- Add a heading like:
  - "Recording History"
- The history list should look secondary, not equal priority to the recorder.
- Existing meeting rows/status badges should remain intact.
- Do not remove existing recording history functionality.

D) Responsive behavior
- On large screens, Calendar + right sidebar should still work.
- On smaller screens, Meetings & Recorder should still appear directly after the calendar content.
- Avoid absolute positioning that breaks scrolling.
- Avoid hardcoded pixel hacks that only work on one screen size.

E) Tests / regression checks
Add tests or DOM/CSS regression checks proving:
1. Meetings & Recorder is rendered directly after the calendar container in page structure.
2. There is no large fixed spacer between calendar and recorder.
3. Empty day calendar does not create excessive vertical blank space.
4. Busy day calendar remains usable without pushing recorder unnecessarily far down.
5. Meeting Recorder action card exists above Recording History.
6. Recorder CTA is visually and structurally distinct from history rows.

F) Verification
After implementation:
- Open Calendar & Meetings as Trevor/admin.
- Confirm on the current day view:
  - Meetings & Recorder appears immediately below the calendar block.
  - No huge blank gap.
  - Recorder card stands out clearly above history.
- Test empty/light day and busy day if mock data exists.
- Test Month, Week, and Day views.
- Confirm no console errors.
- Run relevant tests and report changed files.
