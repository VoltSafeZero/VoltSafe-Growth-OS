oltSafe Cortex is going to become the operating system for the modern marina, the Partnerships section should reflect the ecosystem that surrounds electrification at the dock.

Not just who we sell to.
Who multiplies distribution, adoption, and credibility.

Think of it as the network layer of the business.

Below is the structure I would use.

⸻

Partnerships (VoltSafe Cortex)

1. Strategic Industry Partners

Organizations that influence the marina industry at scale.

These drive credibility, policy awareness, and market access.

Examples:
	•	Marina associations
	•	Marine industry groups
	•	Standards organizations
	•	Port authorities

Typical records inside this category:
	•	Association of Marina Industries
	•	American Boat & Yacht Council
	•	NMMA
	•	Pacific Coast Congress of Harbormasters
	•	Boating Ontario
	•	Washington Maritime Blue

These groups shape marina policy, safety standards, and industry direction.  ￼

Suggested fields:
	•	Organization
	•	Type (Association / Standards / Port Authority)
	•	Membership status
	•	Sponsorships
	•	Speaking opportunities
	•	Events attended
	•	Strategic notes

⸻

2. Technology & Integration Partners

Companies whose software or hardware integrates with VoltSafe.

These create platform expansion and ecosystem lock-in.

Examples:
	•	Dockwa
	•	MarineSync
	•	Quadlogic
	•	Dockmaster
	•	Molo
	•	Slipfinder

These are the existing marina CRM platforms you’re effectively replacing or integrating with.  ￼

Suggested fields:
	•	Integration status
	•	API available
	•	Partner type (CRM / Metering / Payment / IoT)
	•	Joint roadmap
	•	Technical contact

⸻

3. Distribution & Channel Partners

Companies that can sell VoltSafe into large marina networks.

Even if you sell direct initially, these matter long term.

Examples:
	•	Marina construction companies
	•	Dock builders
	•	Electrical contractors
	•	Marine equipment distributors
	•	Boat dealerships

Suggested fields:
	•	Territory
	•	Sales reach (# marinas served)
	•	Certification status
	•	Training completed
	•	Deal registrations

⸻

4. Government & Funding Partners

Programs that fund pilots, trials, and infrastructure upgrades.

Examples:
	•	Innovate BC
	•	NRC IRAP
	•	Ocean Supercluster
	•	Clean energy grants
	•	Port authority innovation programs

These programs already supported VoltSafe pilots and development.  ￼

Fields:
	•	Program name
	•	Funding amount
	•	Grant stage
	•	Deliverables
	•	Reporting schedule

⸻

5. Pilot & Lighthouse Marinas

These are not just customers.

They are market proof.

Examples:
	•	Port of San Diego
	•	Royal Vancouver Yacht Club
	•	Early pilot marinas

Fields:
	•	Slip count
	•	Pedestals deployed
	•	Pilot stage
	•	Case study status
	•	Testimonial potential

⸻

6. OEM & Licensing Partners

Where VoltSafe becomes embedded into other products.

Examples:
	•	Swappable battery companies
	•	EV charging manufacturers
	•	Boat manufacturers
	•	RV infrastructure companies

VoltSafe already has a binding licensing agreement with a global corporation in this category.  ￼

Fields:
	•	License type
	•	Territory
	•	Royalties
	•	Product integration
	•	NDA status

⸻

7. Research & Innovation Partners

Labs and universities helping with next-gen tech.

Examples:
	•	University research labs
	•	Maritime innovation hubs
	•	Accelerator programs
	•	Hardware test labs

Examples VoltSafe already engages with:
	•	Newlab
	•	Vancouver Maritime Centre for Climate  ￼

Fields:
	•	Program type
	•	R&D focus
	•	Prototype stage
	•	IP implications
