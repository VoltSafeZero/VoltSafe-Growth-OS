Build out VoltSafe CMS marketing and campaign capabilities for targeting marina stakeholders.

Context:
VoltSafe sells a smart shore power marina solution that includes a marina pedestal, smart 30A shore power connector, and software. The software is sold as a SaaS monthly subscription. VoltSafe targets marinas and marine infrastructure stakeholders including owners, GMs, harbormasters, dockmasters, deckhands, marine electricians, municipal marina managers, port authorities, yacht clubs, resort marinas, developers, and marina groups.

Goal:
Create a full “Growth / Campaigns” capability inside VoltSafe CMS that helps VoltSafe identify, segment, target, educate, and convert marina stakeholders through campaigns, while tying all engagement back into CRM accounts, contacts, lead stages, opportunities, and follow-up tasks.

This should not be a generic newsletter tool. It should be a marina-specific growth engine.

Build the feature around the idea that VoltSafe sells into a multi-stakeholder buying committee. A marina owner may approve the spend, a GM may drive the project, a harbormaster may feel the operational/safety pain, a marine electrician may validate the technical side, and a port/municipality may control procurement. Campaigns must therefore support both contact-level targeting and account-level buying committee insight.

3. Rogers’ Adoption Curve Mapping

Map Marina Personas to adoption stages.

Recommended mapping:

* Innovators: Developers / New Builds, strategic public infrastructure pilots, premium destination marinas, government/institutional docks
* Early Adopters: Premium Independent Marinas, Marina Groups / Multi-Site Operators, Resort / Destination Marinas, progressive Port Authorities
* Early Majority: Municipal Marinas, larger independent marinas, Yacht Clubs, Working Harbours with known power issues
* Late Majority: Mom & Pop Marinas, smaller budget-constrained marinas, low-tech independents
* Laggards: very small, low-budget, low-urgency marinas with no known infrastructure pain

Prioritization:
Tier 1 — Chase Now:

1. Premium Independent Marinas
2. Marina Groups / Multi-Site Operators
3. Resort / Destination Marinas
4. Developers / New Builds
5. Progressive Port Authorities

Tier 2 — Nurture + Selectively Chase:
6. Municipal Marinas
7. Yacht Clubs
8. Larger Independent Marinas
9. Working Harbours with known power issues

Tier 3 — Do Not Spend Heavy Sales Time Yet:
10. Mom & Pop Marinas
11. Very small marinas under 50 slips
12. Low-budget clubs
13. Marinas with no known infrastructure pain

Strategic rule:
VoltSafe should not initially target every marina. VoltSafe should target marinas where safety, modernization, customer experience, metered billing, or infrastructure leadership already matter.

Early-adopter formula:
High-value marina + clear pain + decision-maker access + modernization mindset

8. Smart Follow-Up Engine

When a contact or account engages, VoltSafe CMS should recommend next actions.

Trigger examples:

* Contact opens 3+ times → Mark as warm
* Contact clicks demo CTA → Create sales task
* Contact replies positively → Move lead stage to Engaged
* No open after 2 emails → Suggest different subject line
* Multiple contacts at same marina engage → Flag account as high-intent
* GM and Harbormaster both engage → Recommend account-level outreach
* Electrician clicks technical PDF → Suggest technical follow-up email
* Owner clicks ROI calculator → Suggest business case email
* Port authority contact clicks infrastructure PDF → Suggest public infrastructure briefing follow-up

Recommended actions should create tasks, suggest emails, update CRM stages, and surface high-intent accounts.

18. Final Product Definition

VoltSafe CMS Campaigns should help us identify, educate, and activate the right marina stakeholders at the right accounts, then automatically turn engagement into sales follow-up.

This should create a marketing system that answers:

* Which marina personas should we target first?
* Which accounts are most ready to adopt?
* Which stakeholders matter inside each marina?
* Which message works for each role?
* Which marinas are heating up?
* Who should sales follow up with next?
* Which campaigns are creating pipeline?

Do not build this as a simple email blast tool. Build it as a marina-specific campaign and buying committee intelligence layer inside VoltSafe CMS.
