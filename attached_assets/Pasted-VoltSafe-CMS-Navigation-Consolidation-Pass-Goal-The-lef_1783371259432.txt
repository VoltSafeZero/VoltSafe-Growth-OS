VoltSafe CMS Navigation Consolidation Pass

Goal:
The left sidebar currently has too many subcategories across Work, Pipeline, Operations, Insights, Ecosystem, Marketing, Capital, Learn, and Admin. This creates cognitive overload. Consolidate related subcategories into fewer, clearer sidebar items while preserving all existing routes and functionality.

Important:
Do not delete any pages.
Do not break existing routes.
Do not remove permissions.
Do not hide pages from users who currently have access.
This is primarily a navigation/UX restructure.

Core UX rule:
The left sidebar should show fewer high-level destinations. Deeper related pages should move into page-level tabs, grouped sections, dropdowns, or command-search destinations.

Mapping instructions:

Work:

- Inbox, team inboxes, email-related work views should live under Inbox & Mail.
- Tasks, Daily Execution, Activity Feed, Digest Settings should live under Tasks & Execution.
- Work Calendar, Calendar, Meeting Notes should live under Calendar & Meetings.
- My Travel should live under Travel.
- Email Signatures, AI Voice Profiles, Digest Settings may live under Personal Settings if not better placed elsewhere.

Pipeline:
- Leads and Accounts should be grouped under Leads & Accounts.
- Contacts can remain its own item.
- Quotes and Renewals should be grouped under Quotes & Renewals.

Operations:

Implementation requirements:
1. Audit current nav config and route mappings.
2. Preserve every existing route.
3. Add grouped navigation entries where needed.
4. If nested submenus are supported, use one extra level only.
5. If nested submenus are not supported, route grouped items to landing pages with tabs/cards for the underlying pages.
6. Add redirects or aliases if needed so old routes still work.
7. Ensure command search can still find all individual pages by their old names.
8. Ensure breadcrumbs show the new grouping clearly.
9. Ensure permissions still apply exactly as before.
10. Ensure hidden or restricted modules remain hidden for unauthorized users.

UX requirements:
- No category should show more than 7 sidebar children if possible.
- Avoid three-level visible sidebar nesting unless absolutely necessary.
- Page-level tabs should be used for deeper related tools.
- Add clear landing pages for grouped items where needed.
- Favorites/recent pages should still work if implemented.
- Hover/click states must remain clear.
