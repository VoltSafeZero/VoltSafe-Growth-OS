Use the audit document:

docs/nav-permissions-audit-2026-06-27.md

Do not perform a full sidebar reorganization. Do not implement role-based nav. Do not redesign the app shell. Do not move large nav groups. Do not touch CURRENTS internals, Gmail/mail-permissions, Polyweather, or unrelated modules.

Goal

Clarify confusing sidebar labels using low-risk, user-facing label-only changes.

Routes, components, permissions, backend behavior, and page functionality must remain unchanged.

Scope

Allowed:

1. Rename confusing nav labels only.
2. Keep route/path unchanged.
3. Keep component/page unchanged.
4. Update tests/source checks for labels.
5. Update obvious user-facing page titles only if they directly match the nav label and are safe.

Not allowed:

1. Moving nav items between groups, except if already done in Phase 1.
2. New top-level groups.
3. Role-based nav.
4. Backend permission changes.
5. New routes/components.
6. Deleting features.
7. Reorganizing More/Library.
8. Changing CURRENTS internals.
9. Changing mail/tracking/Gmail modules.
10. Changing CRM behavior.
