  - Deal

Suggested scoring logic:
- exact contact email match: +45
- existing thread match: +35
- matched company domain (non-generic): +15
- open deal with same contact: +20
- subject contains deal/project name: +15
- participant overlap with recent associated email: +15
- bulk email detected: -60
- auto-generated detected: -50
- internal-only email: -80

Thresholds:
- score >= 90: auto-associate to deal/lead if applicable
- score 70-89: associate to contact/company, suggest lead/deal but do not hard-link aggressively
- score < 70: do not auto-associate to lead/deal
- if obviously junk/internal/bulk: mark ignored

Always prefer precision over over-attachment.
