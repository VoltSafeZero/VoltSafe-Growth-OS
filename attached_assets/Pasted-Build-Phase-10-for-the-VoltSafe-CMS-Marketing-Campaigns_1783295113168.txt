3. Campaign Influence Model

Implement conservative attribution rules.

Direct attribution:

* campaign reply classified as meeting_request → meeting/task attribution
* campaign-created task → task_created attribution
* branch-created task → task_created attribution
* account becomes opportunity within 30 days of campaign engagement → opportunity_created attribution
* proposal sent within 60 days of campaign engagement → proposal_sent attribution
* deal won within 180 days of campaign engagement → revenue_influenced attribution

Influenced attribution:

* any campaign touch/open/click/reply within X days before opportunity creation
* stakeholder from same account engaged before opportunity creation
* multiple campaign touches from same account before stage change

Attribution confidence:

* High: direct reply/task/meeting created from campaign
* Medium: same contact/account engaged within 30 days before opportunity
* Low: account was touched within 90 days, no direct action

Do not overclaim.

11. Safety / Trust Rules

Do not overstate attribution.

Use labels:

* Direct
* Influenced
* Assisted
* Manual
* Low Confidence

Never call influenced pipeline “won revenue” unless deal status is actually won.

If an opportunity is attributed to multiple campaigns, show multi-touch influence instead of double-counting revenue silently.

If revenue is included, show whether totals are:

* unique opportunity value
* multi-touch influenced value
* weighted value
