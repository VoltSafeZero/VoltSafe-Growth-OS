“Investors” is incorrectly nested under Ecosystem. That is wrong because Ecosystem is for partners, associations, dealers, referrals, media, and other relationship groups that help VoltSafe sell, validate, influence, or get introduced.

Investors should only include entities or people who can directly fund VoltSafe or administer funding. The rule is:

“Can this entity write VoltSafe a cheque?”
If yes, it belongs in Capital / Investors.
If no, it belongs elsewhere, usually Ecosystem.
