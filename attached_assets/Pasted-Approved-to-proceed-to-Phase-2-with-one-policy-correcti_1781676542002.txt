Updated VoltSafe Inbox policy:

A message belongs to VoltSafe Inbox if:

(
INBOX
OR CATEGORY_PERSONAL
OR CATEGORY_UPDATES
OR CATEGORY_PROMOTIONS
OR CATEGORY_SOCIAL
OR CATEGORY_FORUMS
)

AND NOT:

* SPAM
* TRASH
* DRAFT

Do NOT use:
AND NOT SENT

SENT-only mail should not appear in Inbox, but SENT+INBOX or SENT+CATEGORY_* must remain visible.

So:

* SENT-only = not inbox
* SENT + INBOX = inbox
* SENT + CATEGORY_* = inbox if not spam/trash/draft
