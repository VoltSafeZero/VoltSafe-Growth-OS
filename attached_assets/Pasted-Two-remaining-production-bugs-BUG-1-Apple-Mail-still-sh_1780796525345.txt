- Clicking the second opens the raw PNG.
- Spark does not show this issue.

This means Watch Demo is still present as an actual attachment part somewhere, not just as a CID related part.

Fix:
1. Inspect raw MIME for the 6:18pm or 6:21pm sent email.
2. Search raw MIME for:
   - Watch Demo.png
   - Content-Disposition
   - Content-ID
   - Content-Type: image/png
3. Ensure the Watch Demo CID image part is inside multipart/related only.
4. Ensure it has:

   - Content-Type: image/png

   - filename="Watch Demo.png"
   - name="Watch Demo.png"
