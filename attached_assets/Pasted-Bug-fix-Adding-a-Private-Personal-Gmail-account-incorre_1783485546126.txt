Bug fix: Adding a Private Personal Gmail account incorrectly overrides/hides VoltSafe work and team inboxes in VoltSafe Mail sidebar.

After adding it, the Mail sidebar now shows:
- trevor@hyalos.com as the main selected account
- burgesstrevor76@gmail.com under Team Inboxes
- missing or hidden trevor@voltsafe.com

This is wrong.

3. PRIVATE INBOXES
   - trevor@hyalos.com

- burgesstrevor76@gmail.com must show under PRIVATE INBOXES, not Team Inboxes, if it is marked private_personal.
