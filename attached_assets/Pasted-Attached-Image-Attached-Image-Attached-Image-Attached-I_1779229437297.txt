Here's what was happening and what's fixed:

The bug: When you clicked "+ Add Contact" on the account's People & Pipeline tab and selected a contact (like Josh Hester), it was correctly adding them to the account's contact panel. But because the link went through the junction table only, Josh's own profile page still showed "Unassigned Contacts" — making it look like nothing happened.

The fix: Now when you link an unassigned contact to an account, the system also sets that account as their home account. So after you add Josh Hester to Safe Harbor Group, his profile will correctly show Safe Harbor Group as his organization.

How to use it:

Open the Safe Harbor Group account → go to the People & Pipeline tab
Click + Add Contact (top right of the Contacts section)
A search popover appears — type Josh's name or leave it blank to browse
Click his name in the list — he's immediately linked
He now appears in the account's contacts panel, and his own contact profile will update to show Safe Harbor Group as his organization
If a contact already belongs to another account, they'll still be linked to Safe Harbor Group in the panel, but their home account won't be changed (only unassigned contacts get promoted).
