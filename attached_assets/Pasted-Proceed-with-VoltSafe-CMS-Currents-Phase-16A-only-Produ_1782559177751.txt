Attachment authorization audit

This is high priority.

Verify:

1. Non-member cannot upload to private channel message.
2. Non-member cannot list private channel message attachments.
3. Non-member cannot download private channel message attachments.
4. Non-member cannot upload/list/download DM attachments.
5. User who left group DM cannot upload/list/download old group DM attachments.
6. Public channel attachments still work.
7. Archived channel upload is blocked.
8. Archived channel download is allowed only for viewers/members.
9. Record Currents attachments still work.
10. Temporary files are cleaned up on authorization failure.
11. Attachment filename/path handling is safe.
12. No file path traversal risk.
13. No objectType/objectId spoof can bypass guards.

Notification privacy audit

Verify:

1. Private channel notifications go only to members.
2. Private channel all-message notifications respect channel preferences.
3. Private channel mention notifications go only to members.
4. Muted private channel suppresses notifications correctly.
5. Non-members cannot receive notification action URLs for private content.
6. DM notifications go only to current members.
7. Group DM notifications skip left members.
8. Added-member notifications are stable and deduped.
9. Notification action URLs do not leak inaccessible content.
10. Notification previews do not expose private content to non-members.
