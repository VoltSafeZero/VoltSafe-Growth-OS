Verify the scoring, joins, filters, UI, permissions, and edge cases are production-safe. Fix any issues found.

1. Backend Service Audit

Audit server/services/account-heat-score.ts.

Verify:

* calculateAccountHeatScore(accountId) handles missing account safely
* accounts with no campaign activity return score 0 or Cold
* null account fields do not crash scoring
* null contact fields do not crash role/persona logic
* score always caps at 100
* score never drops below 0
* heat labels are correct:
    * 80–100 Hot
    * 60–79 Warm
    * 40–59 Nurture
    * 20–39 Low
    * 0–19 Cold / Do Not Chase Yet
* score reasons are human-readable
* negative reasons are human-readable
* recommended_next_action matches the actual reason/score state
* no placeholder/fake engagement data is used
* no benchmark data is used in heat score
* compliance risk signals use real suppression/compliance data only

2. SQL / Join Audit

* accounts
* contacts
* campaign_recipients
* campaign_events
* campaign_suppression
* compliance audit tables if used
* marketing_campaigns
* campaign_emails if used

* account_id filter works
* contacts without account_id handled safely
* deleted/archived campaigns handled appropriately

7. Scoring Validation Scenarios

Create or verify test fixtures for:

A. Cold account

* account exists
* no campaign events
    Expected:
* score 0
* label Cold / Do Not Chase Yet
* empty or “no engagement yet” reason

B. Open-only account

* one contact opened one email
    Expected:
* low but nonzero score

C. Click account

* one contact clicked
    Expected:
* higher than open-only account

D. Multi-stakeholder account

* GM + harbormaster both engage
    Expected:
* multiple engaged contacts bonus
* role reasons present

E. Hot account

* owner/GM clicks and recent engagement
    Expected:
* Hot or Warm depending exact score

F. Compliance-risk account

* unsubscribed/suppressed/spam complaint/expired consent
    Expected:
* negative reasons present
* score penalty applied
* recommended action avoids sending

G. Campaign-scoped heat

* account has heat from Campaign A and Campaign B
    Expected:
* /campaigns/:id/hot-accounts only reflects that campaign’s events
