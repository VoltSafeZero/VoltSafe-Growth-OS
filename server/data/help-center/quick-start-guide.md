# VoltSafe Cortex — Quick Start Guide

> **Who is this for?** Anyone logging in for the first time. Read this before anything else.

---

## What is VoltSafe Cortex?

VoltSafe Cortex is your **all-in-one business command center**. It is where the sales team tracks marina customers, manages quotes, monitors deals, and coordinates tasks — all in one place.

Think of it like a super-powered contact book that also tracks money, tasks, emails, and has an AI assistant built in.

---

## Step 1 — Log In

1. Open your browser and go to the VoltSafe Cortex URL.
2. Type your **email address**.
3. Type your **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

> **New employee?** Ask your Admin for login credentials. They manage user accounts under Admin → Users.

---

## Step 2 — Understand the Screen

When you first log in you will see three main areas:

| Area | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Left sidebar** | Navigation menu — all pages live here |
| **Main area** | The page you are currently viewing |
| **Top bar** | Search button, notifications, your profile, and Cortex AI |

The sidebar is organized into sections:
- **GROWTH OS** — CRM, deals, revenue, partnerships
- **TOOLS** — Support tickets, admin, settings, help

---

## Step 3 — Your Five Most Important Pages

Start by learning these five pages. You will use them every single day.

### 1. Today (`/today`)
Your daily dashboard. Shows:
- Meetings scheduled for today
- Tasks that are due
- Hot leads that need attention
- Suggested next actions

**Use this page every morning.**

### 2. Command Center (`/` — the home page)
The central hub. Shows:
- Pipeline health at a glance
- Urgent actions
- Key metrics for your role

### 3. Opportunities (`/opportunities`)
Your list of active deals. Each row is a marina or customer you are trying to sell to.

**Use this page to see who you should be calling.**

### 4. Tasks Hub (`/execution/tasks`)
Everything you need to do. Tasks are created manually, by automations, or by Cortex AI.

**Check this twice a day — morning and end of day.**

### 5. Gmail Inbox (`/gmail`)
Your work email, connected directly to Cortex. Emails are automatically linked to the right marina/contact.

---

## Step 4 — Talk to Cortex AI

The **Cortex AI assistant** is available on every page. Look for the microphone/chat icon in the top bar.

You can ask Cortex things like:
- *"Show me all hot leads"*
- *"How many deals are in my pipeline?"*
- *"Create a task to follow up with Marina Bay"*
- *"What does weighted pipeline mean?"*

Cortex can read your CRM data and answer questions in plain English. It can also write to the CRM on your behalf.

---

## Step 5 — Your First 15 Minutes

Follow this checklist on your very first day:

- [ ] Log in successfully
- [ ] Open Today page — look at your tasks for today
- [ ] Open Opportunities — look at the list, understand the columns
- [ ] Open Tasks Hub — see if any tasks are assigned to you
- [ ] Open Gmail — check if your email is connected (Settings → My Mailboxes)
- [ ] Ask Cortex one question (try: *"Give me a summary of the pipeline"*)

## Common First-Day Questions

**I can't see some pages.** → Your role has specific permissions. Ask your Admin to check your access level under Admin → Users.

**My email isn't connected.** → Go to Settings → My Mailboxes and connect your Google account.

**I don't know what stage a deal should be in.** → See the Pipeline Stages section in the Full Operations Manual.

**I made a mistake and entered wrong data.** → Click on the record, click Edit, fix it, and save. If you can't edit, you have read-only access.
