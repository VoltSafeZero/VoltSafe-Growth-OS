## Logging In

**What is the login page?**
It is the first page you see. It asks for your email and password.

**Steps:**
1. Open your browser.
2. Go to the VoltSafe Cortex website address.
3. Type your email in the **Email** box.
4. Type your password in the **Password** box.
5. Click **Sign In**.

**What if I forget my password?**
Click **Forgot your password?** below the sign-in button. Your Admin can also reset it for you from Admin → Users.

**What if I see "Unauthorized" or "Access Denied"?**
Your account may not be set up yet. Contact your Admin.

## Command Center

**Route:** `/` (the home page after you log in)

**What is it?**
Your role-specific command center. It is customized based on your role.

**What you will see depends on your role:**
- Sales team: pipeline health, lead count, deal velocity

- Operations: project status, task completion rates

## Accounts

**Route:** `/accounts`

**What is an Account?**
An Account is a **company** or **organization**. For VoltSafe, this is usually a marina.

**What information is stored:**
- Company name
- Address and location
- Contact phone and website
- Account type (Marina, Partner, etc.)
- All linked contacts at that company
- All deals with that company
- All emails sent and received
- All notes and activities

**How to find an account:**
1. Click **Relationships → Organizations** in the sidebar.
2. Use the search bar at the top to type the company name.
3. Click the company name to open its full profile.

**How to create a new account:**
1. Go to Accounts page.
2. Click the **+ New Account** button (top right).
3. Fill in the company name (required).
4. Add address, phone, website (optional but recommended).
5. Click **Save**.

> **Warning:** Always search before creating. Duplicates make data messy.

**How to edit an account:**
1. Open the account profile.
2. Click **Edit** (or the pencil icon).
3. Change what you need.
4. Click **Save**.

**Account Profile tabs:**
- **Overview** — key info and quick stats
- **Timeline** — every activity, email, and note, in order
- **Contacts** — all people at this company
- **Deals/Opportunities** — sales history

## Contacts

**Route:** `/contacts`

**What is a Contact?**
A Contact is a **person**. For VoltSafe, this is usually a marina manager, owner, or buyer.

**What information is stored:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Job title
- Which company (Account) they work at
- All emails with them
- All activities and notes

**How to find a contact:**
1. Click **Relationships → Contacts**.
2. Search by name or email.
3. Click their name to open their profile.

**How to add a new contact:**
1. Click **+ New Contact**.
2. Fill in name and email (required).
3. Link them to an Account (important — always link contacts to companies).
4. Click **Save**.

**How to link a contact to an account:**
1. Open the contact.

3. In the **Account** field, type the company name.
4. Select it from the dropdown.
5. Save.

> **Common mistake:** Creating contacts without linking them to an Account. This makes them "orphan contacts" — they don't show up on the company timeline.

## Opportunities

**Route:** `/opportunities`

**What is an Opportunity?**
An Opportunity is a **potential sale**. It represents one specific deal you are working on.

Each opportunity has:
- A name (usually the marina + what you are selling)
- A stage (where it is in the sales process)
- A value (how much money it could be worth)
- A close date (when you expect to close it)
- A linked Account and Contact

**Opportunity Stages:**

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| New Lead | Just identified, not yet contacted |
| Contacted | First outreach sent or made |
| Qualified | Confirmed they have a real need |
| Proposal Sent | Quote or proposal delivered |
| Negotiation | Discussing terms and pricing |
| Closed Won | Deal signed and won |
| Closed Lost | Deal lost — record the reason |

**How to create an opportunity:**
1. Go to Opportunities.
2. Click **+ New Opportunity**.
3. Fill in: name, stage, value, expected close date.
4. Link to an Account.
5. Save.

**How to move a deal to the next stage:**
1. Open the opportunity.
2. Click the **Stage** dropdown.
3. Select the new stage.
4. Save.

**What does "Stale" mean?**
A deal is **stale** if there has been no activity on it for too long. Stale deals show a warning flag. Fix stale deals by logging an activity or updating the stage.

**How to log an activity on a deal:**
1. Open the opportunity.
2. Scroll to the Timeline section.
3. Click **+ Add Activity**.
4. Choose type: Call, Email, Meeting, Note.
5. Write what happened.
6. Save.

## Pipeline

**Route:** `/pipeline`

**What is the Pipeline?**
The Pipeline page shows all your opportunities in a **visual board**. Each column is a stage. Each card is a deal.

**How to use it:**
- Scan each column to see which stage has the most deals
- Look for deals stuck in the same column for too long
- Drag a card to a new column to change its stage (if you have edit access)

**Pipeline view options:**
- **Board view** — visual columns (default)
- **List view** — table with filters

**Pipeline metrics:**
- **Total pipeline value** — sum of all open deal values
- **Weighted pipeline** — sum of (deal value × close probability)

> **Warning:** The pipeline is only as good as the data in it. Update your deals regularly.

## Quotes

**Route:** `/quotes`

**What is a Quote?**
A Quote is a formal price proposal you send to a marina. It lists the products, quantities, and prices.

**How to create a quote:**
1. Go to Quotes.
2. Click **+ New Quote**.
3. Select the Account (marina) this quote is for.
4. Add products using **+ Add Line Item**.
5. For each line item: choose the product, enter quantity.
6. Review the totals.
7. Click **Send** or **Save as Draft**.

**Quote statuses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not sent yet — still being built |
| Sent | Delivered to the customer |
| Accepted | Customer said yes |
| Declined | Customer said no |

**How to download a quote as PDF:**
1. Open the quote.
2. Click **Download PDF** or **Export**.

**How to accept a quote (when customer confirms):**
1. Open the quote.
2. Click **Mark as Accepted**.

**Common mistake:** Forgetting to update quote status. If a customer says yes, update the status immediately.

## Email Signals and Warmness

**What is Warmness?**
Warmness is a score from 0 to 100. It tells you how "warm" your relationship with an account is — based on recent email activity.

**How it works:**
- High score (70–100) = regular recent contact — relationship is warm
- Medium score (40–70) = some contact but not recent
- Low score (0–40) = little or no recent contact — relationship is cold

**Where to see it:**
- On any Account profile — look for the warmness badge
- In the Accounts list — sortable column

**What to do with it:**
- Focus first on medium-warmness accounts (quick to re-engage)
- Do not let warm accounts go cold — keep in touch
- Cold accounts may need a re-engagement campaign

**Email Signals:**
Signals are alerts based on email patterns. Examples:
- No reply in 14 days
- Email opened multiple times (high interest)
- Unsubscribe detected

## Saved Views

**What is a Saved View?**
A Saved View is a **saved filter configuration**. Instead of setting the same filters every time, you save them once and click one button.

**Example:** You always want to see open opportunities over $50,000 assigned to you. You can save that as "My Big Deals".

**How to create a saved view:**
1. Go to Opportunities, Accounts, or Contacts.
2. Set your filters (stage, value, owner, etc.).
3. Click **Save View** or the bookmark icon.
4. Give it a name.
5. Save.

**How to use a saved view:**
Click its name in the view dropdown at the top of the page.

## Bulk Actions

**What are Bulk Actions?**
Bulk Actions let you do something to **many records at once** instead of one by one.

**Examples:**
- Reassign 20 leads to a new rep
- Mark 10 tasks complete
- Delete 15 duplicate accounts
- Export 50 contacts to CSV

**How to use bulk actions:**
1. Go to any list page (Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Tasks).
2. Check the boxes on the left side of the rows you want.
3. A bulk action toolbar appears at the top.
4. Choose your action (Assign, Delete, Export, etc.).
5. Confirm.

> **Warning:** Bulk delete cannot be undone. Double-check before confirming.

## Automations

**Route:** `/automations`

**What is it?**
Rules that run automatically when something happens in the CRM.

**Examples:**
- When a deal reaches "Proposal Sent" stage → automatically create a follow-up task in 3 days

**How to create an automation:**
1. Go to Automations.
2. Click **+ New Rule**.
3. Choose the trigger (e.g., "Deal stage changes").
4. Set the conditions (e.g., "Stage = Proposal Sent").
5. Set the action (e.g., "Create task").
6. Save and activate.

**Morning (15 min):**
1. Open Today page
2. Check hot tasks and meetings
3. Open Pipeline — any deals that moved overnight?
4. Reply to priority emails in Gmail inbox

**Mid-morning (30–60 min):**
5. Call the top 3 leads from your hot list
6. Log each call as an activity immediately after
7. Update deal stages if any moved

**Afternoon:**
8. Send any pending quotes
9. Complete tasks due today
10. Check Cortex suggestions for any urgent actions

**End of Day (10 min):**
11. Create next-day tasks for anything unfinished
12. Mark completed tasks as done
13. Update any deal notes from the day

1. Review Team Workload for blockers
2. Check active project milestones
3. Review procurement and deployment statuses
4. Handle any overdue tasks
5. Update project steps completed by the field team

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Page shows "Access Denied"**
→ Your permissions do not include that module. Ask your Admin to grant access.

**Problem: My email is not syncing**
→ Go to Settings → My Mailboxes → check if your Google account is connected. Reconnect if needed.

**Problem: I can't find a record I know exists**
→ Try Global Search (`⌘K`). Search by email, phone, or partial name.

**Problem: A deal I moved to "Won" is still showing as open**
→ Refresh the page. If still showing, open the deal and re-save the stage.

**Problem: Cortex AI is not answering correctly**
→ Try rephrasing the question. Be specific: "Show me accounts in British Columbia with no activity in 30 days" works better than "Show old accounts".

**Problem: Duplicate accounts in the system**
→ Go to Data Quality Center. Search for duplicates. Use the merge function to combine them.

**Problem: I sent an email from regular Gmail and it's not in Cortex**
→ Emails sent outside Cortex cannot be auto-imported. Log it manually as an Email activity on the account.

**Problem: A task is marked complete but it shows in my list**
→ Check your active filter. You may have "All tasks" selected — switch to "Open only".

## Glossary

**Account** — A company or organization (usually a marina).

**Activity** — Any logged interaction: call, email, meeting, or note.

**Automation** — A rule that triggers actions automatically based on data changes.

**Board Pack** — An auto-generated leadership report package.

**Campaign** — A bulk email sent to a list of contacts.

**Close Date** — The expected date when a deal will be won or lost.

**Contact** — A person (usually a marina manager or buyer).

**CRM** — Customer Relationship Management. The system for tracking customer data.

**Data Quality** — How clean and complete the data in the system is.

**Deal Velocity** — How fast deals move through the pipeline on average.

**Deployment** — The physical installation of VoltSafe equipment at a marina.

**Executive Copilot** — AI assistant that generates daily business briefs and alerts.

**Forecast** — A prediction of future revenue based on current pipeline.

**Hot Lead** — A lead that scores high on urgency and fit signals.

**Install Workflow** — A step-by-step checklist for an installation project.

**Lead** — An early-stage potential sale. Before it is fully qualified.

**Opportunity** — A qualified potential sale with a value, stage, and close date.

**Pipeline** — All your open opportunities, organized by stage.

**Pipeline Coverage Ratio** — Pipeline value divided by revenue target. Shows if you have enough deals to hit target. A ratio of 3x or higher is healthy.

**Procurement** — Purchasing of equipment and materials for a project.

**Proposal** — A document (usually a quote) sent to a customer outlining what you will deliver and at what price.

**Quote** — A formal price offer sent to a customer.

**Renewal** — When an existing customer extends or upgrades their service.

**Saved View** — A saved filter configuration on a list page.

**Signal** — An automated alert based on CRM data patterns.

**Source Attribution** — Tracking where a lead or deal originally came from.

**Stage** — Where a deal is in the sales process (e.g., Qualified, Proposal Sent, Negotiation).

**Task** — A specific action item assigned to a person with a due date.

**Timeline** — A chronological log of all activity on a record.

**Warmness Score** — A 0–100 score showing how active email engagement is with an account.

**Weighted Pipeline** — Pipeline value adjusted for the probability of each deal closing.

**Win Rate** — The percentage of opportunities you win vs. total opportunities closed.
