### CEO / Admin Role

**What matters most to you:**
- Seeing the full picture of the business at a glance
- Knowing if the team is on track
- Reviewing board-level performance data
- Managing user access and system health

**Your most important pages:**

| Page | Why |
|---|---|
| Executive Dashboard (`/executive-dashboard`) | Top-level KPIs — revenue, pipeline health, deal velocity |
| Revenue Hub (`/revenue`) | Financial performance vs. targets |
| Revenue Simulator (`/revenue-sim`) | "What if" scenario planning |
| Board Pack (`/board-pack`) | Auto-generated board-ready reports |
| Executive Copilot (`/executive-copilot`) | AI daily briefings and alerts |
| Admin → Users (`/admin/users`) | Manage team access |

**Daily habits (15 minutes each morning):**
1. Open Executive Copilot → read today's AI-generated brief
2. Check Revenue Hub → glance at pipeline vs. last week
3. Review any alerts flagged by the system

**Weekly habits:**
- Monday: check pipeline health and forecast
- Friday: review team task completion rates in Team Workload

**What success looks like:**
- You can answer "how is the business doing?" without asking your team
- Pipeline forecast is up to date and trusted
- No data quality issues flagged

**Common mistakes:**
- Skipping the daily brief — the AI catches things humans miss
- Not checking user permissions after onboarding new staff

**KPI ownership:** Overall revenue attainment, pipeline coverage ratio, deal velocity

### Sales Role

**What matters most to you:**
- Knowing which leads to call today
- Moving deals forward
- Sending quotes quickly
- Logging every interaction

**Your most important pages:**

| Page | Why |
|---|---|
| Today (`/today`) | Your daily action list |
| Opportunities (`/opportunities`) | Your entire pipeline |
| Pipeline (`/pipeline`) | Visual board view of deals |
| Quotes (`/quotes`) | Create and send proposals |
| Gmail Inbox (`/gmail`) | Email linked to CRM |
| Tasks Hub (`/execution/tasks`) | Your to-do list |
| Contacts (`/contacts`) | Who to call |
| Accounts (`/accounts`) | Organizations you sell to |

**Daily routine (30 minutes total):**

*Morning (15 min):*
1. Open Today → review hot tasks and meetings
2. Open Opportunities → check any stale deals (yellow/red flags)
3. Open Gmail → reply to any urgent emails

*End of Day (15 min):*
1. Log all calls and meetings as activities on the relevant Opportunity
2. Update deal stages if any deals moved forward
3. Check Tasks Hub — mark completed tasks as done

**Weekly habits:**
- Monday: review full pipeline — identify deals at risk
- Wednesday: check email warmness scores on key accounts
- Friday: update forecast notes on all active deals

**What success looks like:**
- Every deal has a logged activity within the last 7 days
- No opportunities are in "stale" status without a reason
- Quotes are sent within 24 hours of request

**Common mistakes:**
- Not logging calls (makes data useless)
- Skipping deal stage updates (breaks the forecast)
- Forgetting to attach emails to the correct account

**KPI ownership:** New opportunities created, deals closed, quote-to-close ratio, pipeline value

## Training Plans

---

### 30-Minute Onboarding Training Plan

This covers the minimum a new employee needs to function on day one.

**Minutes 0–5: Log in and navigate**
- Log in, walk through the sidebar sections
- Show the Today page and explain what each widget means
- Show the command bar (`⌘K`)

**Minutes 5–10: CRM basics**
- Open Opportunities — explain what a "lead" vs "opportunity" is
- Open one deal — show the timeline, activities, and linked contacts
- Show the difference between an Account and a Contact

**Minutes 10–15: Daily workflow**
- Show how to log an activity (call, meeting, note)
- Show how to create a task and assign it
- Show how to update a deal stage

**Minutes 15–20: Email and communication**
- Show Gmail inbox and how emails link to accounts
- Show how to send an email from inside a deal
- Explain warmness scores briefly

**Minutes 20–25: Quotes and pipeline**
- Show how to create a quote
- Show the pipeline board view
- Explain what stale means and how to fix it

**Minutes 25–30: Cortex AI and help**
- Show how to open and ask the Cortex AI assistant
- Show the Help Center (this page)
- Tell them to ask Cortex any question before asking a colleague

---

### How to Know if Someone is Using the System Correctly

**Good signals:**
- Activities are logged regularly (check the activity timeline on any account they manage)
- Tasks are being completed and new ones created
- Deal stages are moving — not stuck in one stage for weeks
- Emails are associated with the right accounts
- They can navigate independently without being shown where things are

**Warning signs:**
- No activities logged in the past 7 days on their accounts
- All deals at the same stage they were last week
- Email inbox not connected
- They have no tasks assigned or have many overdue tasks
- They regularly ask basic navigation questions after week 2

## Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them

| Mistake | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Not logging calls | Pipeline data becomes unreliable | Make it a daily habit: log every call as an activity |
| Updating deals only at end of month | Forecast is inaccurate all month | Update stages same day they change |
| Using email instead of CRM tasks | Work becomes invisible | Create a task in Cortex for every follow-up commitment |
| Forgetting to link emails to accounts | Email context is lost | Use the Gmail inbox inside Cortex, not external Gmail |
| Not using saved views | Wasting time filtering every time | Save your most-used filters as a named view |
| Creating duplicate accounts | Data quality degrades | Always search before creating. Use Data Quality page to merge |
| Skipping the Today page | Missing urgent items | Make it your browser home page |

## Weekly Team Habits

**Sales team (Monday standup agenda):**
1. Each rep reviews their top 3 deals in pipeline view
2. Flag any deals at risk — agree on action
3. Review last week's activities count — is it enough?
4. Check for stale deals and agree who will follow up

**Operations team (Wednesday check-in):**
1. Open Team Workload — is anyone overloaded?
2. Review active projects for late milestones
3. Check procurement orders and deployments for blockers

**Leadership (monthly):**
1. Open Revenue Simulator — run best/worst/base scenarios
2. Review Board Pack — check auto-generated insights
3. Audit data quality — run Data Quality page and fix issues
