# Storyboard: 02 — Leads, Accounts & Contacts

**Video title:** Your CRM — Leads, Accounts, and Contacts
**Target audience:** Sales reps, SDRs, business development
**User outcome:** Viewer can find a lead, open its profile, navigate to the linked account, and look up a contact
**Recommended duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/02-leads-accounts-contacts.cjs`

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:10] Intro**
> "In this video we'll walk through the core of your CRM:
> Leads, Accounts, and Contacts — and how they connect."

> **[0:11–0:50] Leads**
> "The Leads list is your prospecting workspace.
> Every marina you're tracking before they've committed lives here.
> Use the filters to zero in on the right segment — shore power type, region, or priority."

> **[0:51–1:30] Lead profile**
> "Click any lead to open its profile.
> You'll see the marina's key details, its AI lead quality score,
> the stage it's in, and the suggested next action.
> This tells you exactly what to do before you pick up the phone."

> **[1:31–2:05] Accounts**
> "Once a lead converts — meaning they've signed on or entered a trial —
> they move to Accounts.
> This is your active customer list."

> **[2:06–3:00] Account profile**
> "The Account profile is the richest view in the CRM.
> You'll see all open deals, the full email thread history,
> everyone you know at the marina, and the AI's suggested next step.
> Before any call, start here."

> **[3:01–3:35] Contacts**
> "Contacts are the people behind each account.
> A marina might have a dock master, a general manager, and a finance contact —
> each with their own profile, email history, and relationship warmth score."

> **[3:36–3:55] Outro**
> "Leads become Accounts. Accounts have Contacts.
> In the next video, we'll show you how deals move through the pipeline."
