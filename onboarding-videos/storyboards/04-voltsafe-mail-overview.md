# Storyboard: 04 — VoltSafe Mail Overview

**Video title:** VoltSafe Mail — Your Inbox, Built for Sales
**Target audience:** All users who send and receive email in the CRM
**User outcome:** Viewer can navigate the inbox, find and open messages, use categories, and understand how email links to CRM records
**Recommended duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/04-voltsafe-mail-overview.cjs`

---

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:12] Intro**
> "VoltSafe Mail is your inbox — but purpose-built for marina sales.
> Every email you send or receive is automatically connected to your CRM."

> **[0:13–0:50] Inbox overview**
> "When you open Mail, you see your Smart Inbox.
> Messages are grouped intelligently — Priority, People, Updates, and Notifications —
> so you always know what needs your attention first."

> **[0:51–1:15] Priority tab**
> "The Priority tab is your most important view.
> It surfaces emails from active leads and open deals —
> these are the conversations that can move revenue."

> **[1:16–1:35] People tab**
> "The People tab groups your inbox by contact.
> Great for seeing all correspondence with a specific marina at a glance."

> **[1:36–2:20] Opening a message**
> "Click any message to open the full thread.
> Notice the right-hand panel — it shows you the account, the open deal,
> and the AI's suggested next action.
> You get full context before you type a single word."

> **[2:21–2:55] Reply**
> "Hit Reply to respond directly in the thread.
> VoltSafe automatically appends your signature, logs the send in the CRM,
> and enables open and click tracking."

> **[2:56–3:20] Unread filter**
> "Start every morning with the Unread filter.
> Zero in on what needs a response before anything else."

> **[3:21–3:50] Compose**
> "For new outreach, hit Compose.
> Type a name or account and VoltSafe links the email to the right CRM record instantly."

> **[3:51–4:00] Outro**
> "Your inbox and your CRM, fully in sync.
> Next up: the AI Email Generator."
