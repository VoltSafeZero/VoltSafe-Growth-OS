# Storyboard: 06 — Account Intelligence View

**Video title:** Account Intelligence — Know Everything Before You Call
**Target audience:** Sales reps, account executives, customer success managers
**User outcome:** Viewer can use the account profile as a pre-call research tool — reading activity, people, email history, and next steps before any outreach
**Recommended duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/06-account-intelligence-view.cjs`

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:12] Intro**
> "The most underused habit in sales: reading the account before you call.
> VoltSafe makes it effortless. Here's the account intelligence view."

> **[0:13–0:40] Account header**
> "The header gives you the key facts immediately —
> marina name, type, location, and your current deal stage.
> No hunting around. Everything is surfaced."

> **[0:41–1:00] Quick actions**
> "The quick action buttons let you log a note, create a task,
> or open a deal without navigating away.
> Great for capturing insights during a live call."

> **[1:01–1:45] Intelligence panel**
> "This is the pre-call brief that Cortex generates automatically.
> It shows your champion contact, open opportunities,
> the last activity date, and the AI's recommended next step.
> Read this before every call."

> **[1:46–2:10] Champion card**
> "The champion card highlights your most important contact.
> It shows their name, title, relationship warmth, and the last time you spoke."

> **[2:11–2:45] Activity timeline**
> "The activity timeline is the account's history in chronological order.
> Emails sent, notes logged, deal stage changes — all in one view.
> You'll never start a call without context again."

> **[2:46–3:10] Open opportunities**
> "Open opportunities show you the active deals linked to this account.
> Check the stage, value, and expected close date before you call."

> **[3:11–3:40] Email history**
> "Every email thread with this account appears here.
> You can see what was said, when, and whether the recipient opened or clicked."

> **[3:41–4:00] Notes**
> "Notes are where you capture call outcomes and follow-up context.
> Add a note during or after every interaction.
> Your team sees everything."

> **[4:01–4:10] Outro**
> "Three minutes of account prep before every call.
> That's the habit that separates top reps from average ones.
> VoltSafe makes it take thirty seconds."
