# Storyboard: 03 — Marina Lead Pipeline

**Video title:** How a Marina Lead Moves Through Your Pipeline
**Target audience:** Sales reps, sales managers
**User outcome:** Viewer understands what each pipeline stage means and how to work deals through the funnel
**Recommended duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/03-marina-lead-pipeline.cjs`

---

## Pipeline stages (VoltSafe CRM)

| Stage | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| New Lead | First contact or import — not yet qualified |
| Qualified | BANT confirmed — budget, authority, need, timeline |
| Discovery | Active conversations — site visit or calls underway |
| Pilot Candidate | Marina has agreed to a pilot deployment |
| Proposal | Formal quote submitted |
| Closed Won | Contract signed |
| Closed Lost | Decided not to proceed |

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:12] Intro**
> "In this video we'll walk through the marina lead pipeline —
> how a prospect moves from first contact all the way to Closed Won."

> **[0:13–0:45] Kanban overview**
> "The Pipeline board gives you a visual overview of every active deal.
> Each column is a stage. Each card is a deal."

> **[0:46–2:00] Stage by stage**
> "New Leads are your raw prospects — identified but not yet qualified.
> Once you've confirmed budget, authority, need, and timeline,
> move them to Qualified.
> Discovery means you're in active dialogue — calls, site visits, demos.
> Pilot Candidate is a big moment — the marina has agreed to try VoltSafe.
> Proposal means the quote is submitted. Now we follow up.
> Closed Won — congratulations."

> **[2:01–2:40] Deal card**
> "Click any card to open the full deal record.
> You'll see the deal value, which contacts are involved,
> the expected close date, and the AI-suggested next action."

> **[2:41–3:10] List view**
> "Prefer a spreadsheet view? Switch to List.
> You get the same data in a scannable format — useful for pipeline reviews."

> **[3:11–3:40] Forecast view**
> "The Forecast view breaks down your pipeline against your monthly target.
> Your manager will love this view during one-on-ones."

> **[3:41–3:55] Outro**
> "Keeping your pipeline stages accurate is the single highest-leverage
> thing you can do in the CRM. Next up: VoltSafe Mail."
