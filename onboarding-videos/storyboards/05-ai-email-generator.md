# Storyboard: 05 — AI Email Generator

**Video title:** Cortex AI — Generate Your Next Marina Outreach Email
**Target audience:** Sales reps, account executives, SDRs
**User outcome:** Viewer can use the AI email generator to produce a personalised outreach draft from CRM context, review it, and send with confidence
**Recommended duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/05-ai-email-generator.cjs`

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:12] Intro**
> "Writing personalised outreach for dozens of marinas every week is exhausting.
> VoltSafe's AI assistant, Cortex, does the heavy lifting — here's how."

> **[0:13–0:40] Account profile**
> "Start by opening the account you want to reach out to.
> Before you write a single word, read the intelligence panel.
> Cortex has already summarised the relationship, flagged the champion contact,
> and suggested your next move."

> **[0:41–1:10] Intelligence panel & suggested action**
> "The Suggested Action is Cortex's recommendation based on deal signals —
> last email, open opportunities, days since last contact.
> Use this to decide whether to send a check-in, a proposal follow-up,
> or a new pitch angle."

> **[1:11–1:40] Triggering AI email**
> "Hit 'Suggested Email'.
> Cortex will generate a personalised draft using this account's specific context —
> marina name, deal stage, champion contact, and last activity."

> **[1:41–2:30] Reviewing the draft**
> "The draft appears in seconds.
> Read it through — is the tone right? Are the details accurate?
> You can regenerate with different instructions, adjust the length,
> or apply your personal voice profile for a more consistent style."

> **[2:31–3:10] Compose for final review**
> "When you're happy with the draft, click 'Use This Email'.
> It opens in Compose with the subject line and body pre-filled.
> Make any final tweaks, add attachments, and send when ready."

> **[3:11–3:25] Outro**
> "From CRM context to personalised outreach in under 60 seconds.
> That's the AI Email Generator. Next: the Account Intelligence View."
