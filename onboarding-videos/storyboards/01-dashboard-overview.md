# Storyboard: 01 — VoltSafe CMS Dashboard Overview

**Video title:** Welcome to VoltSafe CMS — Your Sales Ecosystem
**Target audience:** New sales reps, SDRs, account executives
**User outcome:** Viewer can navigate confidently to any major area of VoltSafe CMS within their first session
**Recommended duration:** 3–4 minutes
**Script file:** `onboarding-videos/scripts/01-dashboard-overview.cjs`

## Suggested voiceover script

> **[0:00–0:12] Intro**
> "Welcome to VoltSafe CMS — the growth operating system built for marina sales teams.
> In this video we'll take a 3-minute tour of the whole platform."

> **[0:13–0:35] Home / Command Center**
> "When you log in, you land on your personalised command centre.
> This page adapts to your role — a sales rep sees their pipeline and tasks,
> an executive sees company-wide KPIs."

> **[0:36–1:00] Executive Dashboard**
> "The Executive Dashboard gives leaders a real-time view of bookings,
> pipeline value, and team performance — all in one place."

> **[1:01–1:20] Leads**
> "Leads are the lifeblood of your prospecting work.
> Every marina you're tracking before they become a customer lives here."

> **[1:21–1:40] Accounts**
> "Once a lead converts, it becomes an Account.
> Accounts have a full intelligence profile: contacts, emails, notes, and next steps."

> **[1:41–2:00] Pipeline**
> "The Pipeline is your deal-flow view.
> Drag deals between stages or switch to list view for a quick scan."

> **[2:01–2:25] VoltSafe Mail**
> "VoltSafe Mail connects your inbox directly to your CRM.
> Every email you send or receive is automatically linked to the right account."

> **[2:26–2:50] AI Copilot**
> "Cortex, our AI assistant, does the heavy lifting.
> It suggests who to follow up with, drafts your next email, and flags deals at risk."

> **[2:51–3:00] Outro**
> "That's your 3-minute tour. In the next video, we'll go deep on Leads,
> Accounts, and Contacts."
