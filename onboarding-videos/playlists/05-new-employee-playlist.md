# Playlist 05 — New Employees (Full Onboarding)

**Audience:** Anyone joining VoltSafe in a role that touches the CRM — sales, customer success, support, or operations
**Estimated total time:** ~26 minutes
**Format:** Complete all videos in order during your first week

---

## What you should understand by the end

- The complete layout and purpose of VoltSafe CMS
- How leads, accounts, and contacts are structured and how they connect
- How the marina sales pipeline works end to end
- How to research an account before a call or email
- How to use the AI email generator for personalised outreach
- How your inbox is connected to your CRM records

---

## This is your week-one training path

Complete this playlist before your first pipeline review, customer call, or outbound email. It replaces a 2-hour screen share walkthrough and gives you something you can rewatch at your own pace.

---

## Recommended viewing order

| # | Video | Duration | Notes |
|---|-------|----------|-------|

## Suggested weekly schedule

| Day | What to watch | What to do after |
|-----|--------------|-----------------|
| Day 1 | Dashboard Overview | Log in, explore the nav, find your name in the user menu |
| Day 2 | Leads, Accounts & Contacts | Find the accounts assigned to you or your team |
| Day 3 | Marina Lead Pipeline | Shadow a pipeline review or read through open deals |
| Day 4 | Account Intelligence View | Open 3 accounts and read the intelligence panel on each |
| Day 5 | AI Email Generator + VoltSafe Mail | Draft a test email using AI; reply to one inbound in Mail |

## After the playlist — your next steps

1. **Book a 30-minute check-in** with your manager to walk through one live account together
2. **Complete your first real task** — add a note to an account after a call
3. **Join the next pipeline review** — you now have the context to follow the conversation
4. **Ask Cortex AI** a question in the AI Copilot — see what it surfaces for your accounts
5. **Read the storyboards** (`onboarding-videos/storyboards/`) for the videos most relevant to your role — they contain deeper context and voiceover scripts that function as reference guides
