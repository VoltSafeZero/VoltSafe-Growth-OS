# Playlist 01 — Sales Team

**Audience:** Account executives, SDRs, business development reps
**Estimated total time:** ~26 minutes
**Format:** Watch in order — each video builds on the previous

---

## What you should understand by the end

- How to find and qualify a marina lead
- How to move a deal through every pipeline stage
- How to use the account intelligence view before every call
- How to generate a personalised AI email from CRM context
- How your inbox connects to your CRM records

---

## Recommended viewing order

| # | Video | Duration | Why it's in this order |
|---|-------|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Dashboard Overview** (`01`) | ~3.5 min | Orient yourself — understand the full layout before going deep |
| 2 | **Leads, Accounts & Contacts** (`02`) | ~4.5 min | The CRM fundamentals every rep needs first |
| 3 | **Marina Lead Pipeline** (`03`) | ~4.5 min | Understand deal stages before working your first lead |
| 4 | **Account Intelligence View** (`06`) | ~4.5 min | Learn to research an account before every call |
| 5 | **AI Email Generator** (`05`) | ~4.5 min | Generate personalised outreach from the account you just researched |
| 6 | **VoltSafe Mail Overview** (`04`) | ~4.5 min | Connect your inbox to your CRM workflow |

---

## "Skip this if…"

| Video | Skip if… |
|-------|---------|
| Dashboard Overview | You've used VoltSafe CMS before and know your way around |
| Leads, Accounts & Contacts | You already manage a book of business in VoltSafe and know the CRM layout |
| Marina Lead Pipeline | You only do inbound — you're not managing a outbound pipeline |
| Account Intelligence View | You're watching the Sales playlist out of order — don't skip this one |
| AI Email Generator | You prefer to write all outreach manually (still worth 5 minutes) |
| VoltSafe Mail Overview | Your team manages email outside VoltSafe and you don't use the inbox |

---

## "Watch this before doing…"

| Before you… | Watch… |
|-------------|--------|
| Make your first cold call to a marina | Account Intelligence View (`06`) |
| Send your first outreach email | AI Email Generator (`05`) + VoltSafe Mail Overview (`04`) |
| Update a deal stage in your pipeline | Marina Lead Pipeline (`03`) |
| Add a new lead from a trade show | Leads, Accounts & Contacts (`02`) |
| Join your first pipeline review meeting | Marina Lead Pipeline (`03`) |

---

## Follow-up actions after watching

1. Log in and find your assigned leads in the Leads list
2. Open one account profile and read the intelligence panel — does it make sense?
3. Use the AI Email Generator on one account and review the draft
4. Send one real email from VoltSafe Mail (make sure demo mode is off)
5. Ask your manager to do a 15-minute pipeline walkthrough using the kanban view
