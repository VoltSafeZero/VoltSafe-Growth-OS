# Playlist 04 — Support & Admin

**Audience:** Customer success managers, support reps, CRM administrators, operations
**Estimated total time:** ~17 minutes
**Format:** Watch in order — admin users need the full platform context before managing records

---

## What you should understand by the end

- How the CRM is structured and how records relate to each other
- How to find an account and use the intelligence view to handle a support inquiry
- How VoltSafe Mail works and how to respond to inbound customer emails
- What the leads and contacts system looks like so you can manage data quality

---

## Recommended viewing order

| # | Video | Duration | Why |
|---|-------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Dashboard Overview** (`01`) | ~3.5 min | Understand the full platform before managing parts of it |
| 2 | **Leads, Accounts & Contacts** (`02`) | ~4.5 min | You'll add, merge, and maintain CRM records — this is the foundation |
| 3 | **VoltSafe Mail Overview** (`04`) | ~4.5 min | Support reps need to handle inbound email; admins need to understand the mailbox setup |
| 4 | **Account Intelligence View** (`06`) | ~4.5 min | When a customer calls with an issue, this is where you look first |

---

## "Skip this if…"

| Video | Skip if… |
|-------|---------|
| Dashboard Overview | You've been given a platform walkthrough and already know the layout |
| Leads, Accounts & Contacts | You don't manage CRM data — you only respond to tickets |
| VoltSafe Mail Overview | Your team uses a separate helpdesk (e.g. Zendesk) and doesn't touch VoltSafe Mail |
| Account Intelligence View | You only handle internal admin and don't have customer contact |

---

## "Watch this before doing…"

| Before you… | Watch… |
|-------------|--------|
| Add or merge a contact record | Leads, Accounts & Contacts (`02`) |
| Respond to a customer email in VoltSafe | VoltSafe Mail Overview (`04`) |
| Look up a customer's history during a support call | Account Intelligence View (`06`) |
| Set up a new user account (admin only) | Dashboard Overview (`01`) first, then ask your lead admin for the user management guide |

---

## Admin-specific tips

- **Data quality** is your responsibility — watch the Leads, Accounts & Contacts video carefully for how records link together
- **Duplicate accounts** can be merged using the Duplicate Merge Engine (covered in a future video)
- **Email accounts** — mailbox setup and connection status is managed in Settings (covered in a future video)
- **User permissions** — each user's section access is set in their profile (covered in a future video)

---

## Follow-up actions after watching

1. Log in and navigate to Accounts — confirm you can find any account by name
2. Open an account's intelligence view and check the activity timeline
3. Open VoltSafe Mail and confirm your mailbox is connected and visible
4. Ask your team lead: which support workflows go through VoltSafe vs. your helpdesk?
