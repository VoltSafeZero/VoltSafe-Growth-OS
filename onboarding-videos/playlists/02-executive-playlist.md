# Playlist 02 — Executives & Leadership

**Audience:** Founders, VP Sales, VP Customer Success, General Managers
**Estimated total time:** ~17–21 minutes (core: 17 min / with mail: 21 min)
**Format:** Watch core videos in order; Mail is optional

---

## What you should understand by the end

- What the Executive Dashboard shows and how to read it
- How the pipeline is structured and where revenue is at risk
- How account intelligence drives pre-call preparation for your reps
- How the AI email system works and what it produces
- (Optional) How VoltSafe Mail connects to the CRM

---

## What executives typically ask after watching

- "How do I pull a pipeline report for the board?" → Ask your admin to export from the Forecast view (covered in Marina Lead Pipeline video)
- "Can I see all reps' activity in one place?" → Yes — the Executive Dashboard shows team-wide KPIs
- "How do I know if reps are using the AI features?" → Cortex activity is logged per user — ask your system admin

---

## Follow-up actions after watching

1. Open the Executive Dashboard and confirm the KPIs match your mental model
2. Review your team's pipeline in kanban view before your next one-on-one
3. Pull up one account profile alongside your rep — verify they're using the intelligence panel
4. Ask your team: are they using the AI email generator? If not, this is your coaching moment
