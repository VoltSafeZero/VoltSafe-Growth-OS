# Playlist 03 — Marina Operators

**Audience:** Marina managers, harbour masters, on-site operators, dock staff
**Estimated total time:** ~8 minutes (current videos) + future videos TBD
**Format:** Short — only the videos directly relevant to day-to-day marina operations

---

## What you should understand by the end

- How VoltSafe CMS is laid out and where marina-relevant information lives
- How to communicate with the VoltSafe team via VoltSafe Mail (if your marina uses it for customer-facing communication)

---

## Context for this playlist

Most marina operators interact with VoltSafe through a **separate boater-facing interface**, not the full internal CRM. This playlist covers the parts of VoltSafe CMS that are relevant to a marina operator — and is intentionally short. As marina-specific video modules are recorded, this playlist will expand significantly.

---

## Recommended viewing order

| # | Video | Duration | Priority | Why |
|---|-------|----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Dashboard Overview** (`01`) | ~3.5 min | Core | Understand what the platform is before diving into marina-specific features |
| 2 | **VoltSafe Mail Overview** (`04`) | ~4.5 min | Conditional | Watch only if your marina communicates with boat owners or VoltSafe via the CRM inbox |

---

## "Skip this if…"

| Video | Skip if… |
|-------|---------|
| Dashboard Overview | Your marina only uses the boater app and you have no login to VoltSafe CMS |
| VoltSafe Mail Overview | Your marina does not use VoltSafe Mail for customer or support communication |

---

## "Watch this before doing…"

| Before you… | Watch… |
|-------------|--------|
| Log in to VoltSafe CMS for the first time | Dashboard Overview (`01`) |
| Communicate with a boat owner via VoltSafe | VoltSafe Mail Overview (`04`) |

---

## Follow-up actions after watching

1. Log in and confirm you can navigate to the sections relevant to your marina
2. If your marina uses VoltSafe Mail, open your inbox and verify messages are visible
3. Contact your VoltSafe account manager if you're unsure which features apply to your role
